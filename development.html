Seven Cures for a Lean Purse

What the Chapter is About

This chapter teaches us save and control our spending. The line that stick out to me was "Budget thy expenses that thou mayest have coins to pay for thy necessities, to pay for thy enjoyments and to gratify thy worthwhile desires without spending more than nine tenths of thy earnings." It highlight the importance of budgeting your money and you need to separate your needs from your wants.

How Can I Apply It To My Life

As a university student, knowing how to manage and budget your money is important. I prioritize my school fee and other school expense before making any unnecessary spending.
